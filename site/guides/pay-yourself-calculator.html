Pay yourself calculator: median and worst honest month

Type your last 6 to 8 months of profit after costs, separated by spaces or commas. The middle value and the floor tell you what a safe draw looks like.

What it computes

The median of the months you enter (the middle value, immune to one great month) and the worst honest month. The full reasoning lives in the pay yourself guide.
