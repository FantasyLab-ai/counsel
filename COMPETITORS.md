## (b) Competitor capsules (key claims, sourced)

**QuickBooks + Intuit Assist** — $19–137.50/mo; Assist bundled free. Since
Jul 1 2025: agentic AI, daily briefings, "proposed action plans" — the giant IS
moving toward operationalization, via LLM narrative (GenOS), **zero quantified
uncertainty**. Mobile: 4.7/5 across ~251k ratings BUT feature-reduced (only P&L
+ balance sheet on mobile; AR/AP/inventory missing), update regressions, 1–3hr
support calls. Trustpilot 1.1/5 (unclaimed, complaint-skewed): price hikes,
upselling, support rage. *Threat + validation in one.*

**Xero (+ Syft/Analytics Plus)** — $25–90/mo. Trend analysis + cash-flow
forecast, no bands, no causal, OCR "AI" that reviewers say extracts nothing.
Bimodal Trustpilot (65% 5★ / 16% 1★): repeated ~10%/yr price hikes, no inbound
phone support. **Their 5-star engine is human 1:1 onboarding calls — steal it.**

**Zeni** — $549–799/mo AI bookkeeping + human team; "AI CFO Agent" does runway/
burn prescriptively but no confidence/uncertainty anywhere; targets VC-backed
startups; G2 4.5 but polarized — complaints are service-layer (contracts,
support hours, an alleged unauthorized $5k deduction). Not our segment; proof
the "AI CFO" phrase sells.

**Digits** — from $200/mo (CPA bundle $350). "Ask Digits" chat over books; "AI
assists, does not execute"; practitioner review: dashboards impressive, **"does
not tell owners what to do or flag strategic problems."**

**Puzzle** — $25–300/mo; AI-native ledger, ~98% auto-categorization
(self-claimed, no error bars); burn/runway dashboards; partner-channel;
startups not Main Street.

**Pilot / Bench** — human bookkeeping: Pilot $199–599/mo books, CFO services
$1,875–5,625/mo (our price ceiling to undercut). Bench collapsed Dec 2024 —
cautionary tale for human-heavy models.

**Float / Fathom / Finmark / Dryrun** — $35–299/mo forecasting tools. Float:
3-yr forecasts, "basic ML," G2 reviewer: *"I just don't feel it provides any
value... there's nothing to help me DO anything."* Fathom: AI commentary
narratives + 50 KPIs — a reporting layer. **Finmark: Monte Carlo + sensitivity
+ driver correlation — the only formal-uncertainty tool — but chart-first,
startup-first.** Dryrun: scenario breadth, AI only for validation.

**Jobber / Housecall Pro** (landscaper/contractor dailies) — $39 / $59–399/mo.
Basic revenue reports; no job/tech profitability, no marketing ROI; a
fractional-CFO reviewer: field-service platforms are **"data-collection tools
rather than decision engines"** — owners exported to spreadsheets. That
spreadsheet IS our CSV drop-in.

**ServiceTitan** (enterprise reference) — ~$245–350/tech/mo + $5–18k setup,
$48–96k Yr-1 for a 10-tech shop; analytics rated 8.3–8.4/10 and owners call
reporting core to running the business; 12–16 wk onboarding; **offline-first
mobile is a praised feature** (we already do this). Proves trades value deep
analytics — at a price 100× our target's budget.

**Toast / MarginEdge** (restaurant dailies) — Toast: live descriptive
dashboards + **automated nightly email digest** (steal the habit mechanism);
no forecasting/uncertainty/recommendations anywhere on the reporting page.
MarginEdge: $330/loc/mo real-time food cost, recipe costing, OCR invoices —
descriptive cost ops, no prediction; complaints: long onboarding, peak-time
lag; strengths: POS-native integrations (Toast/Square/Clover/TouchBistro) +
multi-location consolidation.

**EverBee / eRank / Craftybase** (Etsy dailies) — $29.99/mo Pro; product-level
*estimates* from public data with acknowledged accuracy drift and **no
confidence quantification; Chrome-extension only, no mobile app** — an
on-the-go maker has nothing. eRank = SEO. Neither touches cash flow, restock
math, or marketing ROI.

**Lili / Found / Novo** (solopreneur banking) — $0–55/mo. Lili's intelligence =
expense buckets + tax estimate, but it **auto-sets-aside the tax percentage —
a tool that ACTS on an insight** (steal). Cash deposit caps ($1,500/day) hurt
food trucks. No forecasting or analytics anywhere.

**Ramp/Brex/Mercury** — spend-side automation + AI coding agents; free-to-$15/
user; banking rails; not forward-looking owner analytics.

## (c) The 10 gaps Counsel exploits (ranked by defensibility)

1. **Quantified uncertainty as product identity** — nobody at any price ships
   confidence bands as the default language (Finmark has the tooling, buried in
   charts for startups). Cultural + technical moat; incumbents can't adopt
   "we might be wrong" without breaking their marketing.
2. **Causal "was it worth it?"** — event-study on the owner's own decisions.
   Absent from every tool at every tier, including enterprise.
3. **Graded advice (the Decisions tracker)** — no product grades its own
   recommendations against outcomes. Accountability is our trust flywheel.
4. **Payroll-anchored cash-crunch early warning** — 39% of SMBs hold <1 month
   reserves; payroll is the #1 48-hour panic trigger (51.3%). Float/Fathom
   forecast lines; nobody alarms against the owner's actual payroll dates
   (our Monte Carlo already does).
5. **On-device privacy** — top-2 SMB AI concern (privacy + accuracy) per the
   BILL/SMB survey; every competitor is cloud + LLM. Only we can say "your
   books never leave your phone" — literally.
6. **The analysis layer OVER vertical systems of record** — Jobber/Toast/Etsy
   own the workflow and abandon the owner at "here's a chart." We read their
   exports/APIs and answer "so what do I do?" No incumbent can do this across
   verticals without cannibalizing their own upsells.
7. **Price-power from own history (elasticity)** — nobody, anywhere, at SMB
   tier. Prints money for the user; deeply hard to copy without real stats.
8. **Restock math for micro-retail (newsvendor)** — MarginEdge does food-cost
   ops at $330/loc/mo; nothing exists at $20–50 for makers/trucks/retail.
9. **Marketing lift truth** — event-study vs platform-flattering ROI numbers;
   no SMB tool measures "did the post move money" honestly.
10. **Honest peer benchmarks with intervals** — gestured at by incumbents,
    never quantified; becomes a network moat as the cohort grows.

## (d) The 5 things competitors do better — steal list

1. **Xero's 1:1 onboarding call** — their single biggest 5-star driver. Ours:
   a white-glove first-run (guided connect/CSV session, human or scripted).
2. **Toast's nightly digest email** — the habit loop. Ours: the Morning Brief
   as push/email once hosted (already designed in Settings).
3. **Lili's auto tax set-aside** — acting on the insight, not just showing it.
   Ours: "move it for me" actions once banking connects (start with the tax pot).
4. **MarginEdge's POS-native integration breadth + multi-location view** —
   integration-first per vertical; consolidation patterns.
5. **QBO mobile's utility hooks (receipt snap, invoicing) + ServiceTitan's
   offline field mobile** — daily-utility features earn the open; keep our
   offline-first advantage loud.

Also validated: Intuit's agents now deliver "daily briefing + recommended
actions" — the giant legitimizes the category; our counter is *verifiable*
math, privacy, price, and a mobile app that isn't feature-cut.

## (e) Persona fit notes

- **Food truck** — Square data + cash-heavy (Lili's deposit caps hurt);
  payroll-trigger stats hit hardest here; weekday/weather rhythm = our
  seasonality + cash sentry. Wedge: cash-crunch warning + day-of-week truth.
- **Landscaper / contractor** — Jobber/HCP leave analysis to spreadsheets
  (their words); wedge: CSV drop-in of Jobber exports → job profitability,
  quote-to-cash, staffing-to-demand. ServiceTitan proves the appetite.
- **Restaurant** — Toast descriptive + MarginEdge at $330/loc; wedge: cost +
  cash + season-adjusted daily read at 1/10 the price; steal nightly digest.
- **Etsy maker** — EverBee = estimates, Chrome-only, no mobile; wedge: honest
  numbers on a phone + restock math + post-lift truth; Craftybase users
  already track COGS — CSV imports cleanly.
- **All five** — privacy + accuracy are their stated top AI concerns; the
  glass box + on-device story is aimed at exactly that.

## Pricing benchmarks & model

- Personas already pay: Jobber $39, EverBee $30, Lili $15–35, QBO solo $20–38.
- Depth ceiling: MarginEdge $330/loc; AI-CFO services $549–5,625/mo.
- Fintech freemium converts ~4.1% free→paid; opt-out trials 49.9% vs opt-in
  17.8% (3× more trials). Implication: **free tier = demo + CSV drop-in**
  (on-device, costless to serve), **$29–49/mo = connectors + alerts + Packet**,
  positioned as "a fractional CFO for 1% of the price."
- 87% of SMBs shopping integrated financial software in 12–24 mo; cash-flow
  visibility is the top purchase driver — lead marketing with the cash sentry.
